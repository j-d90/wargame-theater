# Desert Storm scenario sources

- Order of battle: Wikipedia, 'Liberation of Kuwait campaign order of battle' (retrieved 2026-10-19), which notes that details of the Iraqi order of battle are disputed; division count cross-checked with GlobalSecurity.org 'Iraqi ground forces 1991'. Positions are approximate, placed by corps sector.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberation_of_Kuwait_campaign_order_of_battle
  https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/world/iraq/ground-91.htm
- Unit stats are abstract game values chosen for play balance (strength, quality, supply use, speed). They are not real equipment, manpower or performance data.
- Abstractions: Saudi, Kuwaiti and other GCC brigades in the two Joint Forces Commands are combined into division-sized task forces; the Syrian division is listed under "other coalition contingents"; separate Iraqi brigades are omitted.
- Coalition positions are for 17 January 1991, before the corps moved west; the AI's assembly areas reproduce that shift.
